Cafe Profitability & Cost Control Analysis
Power BI | DAX | Power Query | Business Analysis

Overview:

This project analyses the operating performance of a cafe to understand why growth in orders and revenue is not translating into stronger profitability.

The business context comes from my own experience running a cafe after leaving my previous role in legal and contract lifecycle management. While the dataset used in this portfolio project has been constructed for analytical purposes, the business problem and operating context are based on the type of decisions I encountered while running the business.

The central question is simple: If revenue and orders are growing, why isn't profitability improving at the same rate?

We are trying to explore how simple cost cutting can impact the business in a manner that drives profitability. 

Key Findings:

The analysis identifies a significant gap between current expenses and the defined target expense level.

Metric	Value
Current Expenses = ₹3.98M
Target Expenses = ₹2.14M
Potential Expense Reduction = ₹1.84M

The analysis indicates that the largest cost-control opportunities are concentrated in categories such as raw materials, staff salaries, rent and packaging.

Reducing costs should not mean cutting expenditure indiscriminately. The objective is to identify expenses that are disproportionately high relative to revenue and prioritise areas where efficiency improvements can increase profitability without unnecessarily affecting sales or customer experience.

The analysis identifies where expenses exceed the defined target and provides a measurable basis for prioritising cost reduction.

Recommendations:

The business should focus first on the largest and most controllable expense variances rather than attempting broad cost cutting.
Raw materials, staffing, rent and packaging should be reviewed against their contribution to revenue and operating requirements.
The objective is to reduce unnecessary cost while protecting the expenditure that supports sales, product quality and customer experience.
Once the cost base is brought closer to target, ongoing monitoring of expense-to-revenue ratios can help prevent costs from rising faster than revenue again.
